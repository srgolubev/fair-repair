Terms and Conditions

At Fair Repair Appliance Service, we take pride in providing our customers with high-quality repair services. Please read the following terms and conditions carefully before scheduling a repair with us.

1. Payment

Payment for all repairs is due in full upon completion of the repair/diagnostic. We accept cash, checks, and major credit cards. Processing fee to be applied to all transactions using credit/debit cards. We do not offer any payment plans or financing options at this time.

2. No Refund Policy

We do not offer refunds for any services performed. If we repair your appliance and it works properly after the repair, we consider our job done. However, if you experience any issues with your appliance within 90 days of the repair, please contact us immediately to schedule a follow-up repair. We will make every effort to resolve any issues with your appliance under our warranty.

3. Warranty

We offer a 90-day warranty on all repairs. This warranty covers any parts and labor associated with the original repair. If the same issue reoccurs within 90 days of the original repair, we will repair it again at no cost to you. No warranty provided for parts or jobs with parts provided by the customer or the other sources.

4. Exclusions

Our warranty does not cover any new issues that arise after the original repair, any damage caused by misuse or neglect of the appliance, or any issues caused by external factors such as power outages or natural disasters.

5. Cancellation

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please contact us as soon as possible. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment may be subject to a cancellation fee.

By scheduling a repair with us, you agree to these terms and conditions. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us. We appreciate your business and look forward to serving your appliance repair needs.
